Who We Are

Mission & Vision

Mission: To build a peaceful, prosperous, and equitable South Sudan by empowering communities through sustainable agriculture, clean water and sanitation, conflict resolution, and the protection of women's and youth rights.

Vision: A South Sudan where every community thrives in peace, with access to clean water, food security, and equal opportunities for women and youth.

Our Approach

Strategic Framework

PADRCO's strategy is built on three interconnected pillars that guide every program and initiative we undertake.

Step 1

Community-Led Assessment

We begin by working directly with communities to identify their most pressing needs, assets, and aspirations — ensuring that every intervention is locally driven and culturally appropriate.

Step 2

Partnership & Capacity Building

We collaborate with local CSOs, government agencies, and international partners to strengthen local capacity, share knowledge, and amplify our collective impact.

Step 3

Sustainable & Inclusive Delivery

We implement programs that are environmentally sustainable, socially inclusive, and designed to create lasting change — with a special focus on women, youth, and persons with disabilities.

Our Pillars

What We Stand For

PADRCO is dedicated to four interconnected pillars that form the foundation of a resilient and just society.

Peace

Promoting dialogue, conflict resolution, and social cohesion in communities across South Sudan.

WASH

Ensuring access to clean water, proper sanitation, and hygiene education for all.

Agriculture

Empowering farmers with sustainable techniques, seeds, and tools for food security.

Women & Youth

Advocating for the rights, education, and economic empowerment of women and young people.
