How It Works

Our Proven Process

From initial consultation to the final splash, we guide you through every step of creating your dream pool with transparency and expertise.

1

Consultation & Design

Phase One

We start with an in-depth consultation to understand your vision, assess your property, and create a custom design. Our 3D renderings bring your dream pool to life before construction begins.

2

Permits & Engineering

Phase Two

We handle all necessary permits and engineering with the City of Houston and surrounding municipalities. Full compliance with local codes and regulations.

3

Excavation & Construction

Phase Three

Our skilled crews begin excavation and structural construction using premium materials and industry-leading techniques with a clean, organized jobsite.

4

Finishes & Features

Phase Four

We install premium finishes, tile, coping, plumbing, electrical systems, and custom features like waterfalls, lighting, or outdoor kitchens.

5

Final Inspection & Fill

Phase Five

After thorough quality checks and final inspections, we fill your pool and balance the chemistry. We walk you through every system and feature.

6

Enjoy Your Paradise

Phase Six

Dive in and enjoy your new pool! We provide comprehensive maintenance guidance and remain available for any future service needs.

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Get answers to the most common questions about pool construction in Houston, TX. Have more questions? Contact us directly.

A typical custom pool construction in the Houston area takes 8–14 weeks from groundbreaking to completion, depending on the complexity of the design, weather conditions, and permit approvals. We'll provide a detailed timeline during your consultation so you know exactly what to expect.

Custom pool costs in Houston vary widely based on size, design complexity, materials, and features. A basic custom pool typically starts around $65,000, while luxury pools with infinity edges, spas, and outdoor kitchens can range from $120,000 to $250,000+. We provide detailed, transparent quotes.

Absolutely. Lone Pool handles all permits, engineering documents, and municipal inspections required for pool construction in Houston and surrounding areas. This includes plumbing permits, electrical permits, and barrier/fencing compliance.

We offer a comprehensive warranty package that includes a lifetime structural warranty on the pool shell, 3-year warranty on plaster and tile finishes, and 1-year warranty on all equipment and workmanship.

Yes! We partner with leading home improvement lenders to offer competitive financing options for your pool project. Terms typically range from 5–20 years with rates starting as low as the current market allows.

In Houston, pool construction can happen year-round. However, the best time to start is typically late fall or early spring to avoid peak summer demand and ensure your pool is ready by swimming season.
